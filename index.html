Five Square

How to play

Guess the hidden word in 6 tries.

  • Each guess must be a valid 5-letter word. Tap Enter to submit.
  • After each guess the tiles change colour to show how close you were.

Repeated letters are coloured fairly: exact matches go first, then a letter is only marked as in the word as many times as it actually appears.

Modes

Daily: one word per day, the same for everyone, with a new word at local midnight. Your streak counts daily games only.

Practice: unlimited random words. Tap New word whenever you like. Practice doesn't affect your daily stats.
